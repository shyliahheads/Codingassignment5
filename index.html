About This Recipe

Homemade hamburgers are a classic meal that you can customize with your favorite toppings. Start with seasoned ground beef, cook the patties thoroughly, and finish with a soft bun.

Recipe style: Classic American-inspired burger

Difficulty: Beginner-friendly

Recipe Details

Quick Facts

Preparation: 15 minutes

Cooking: 10–15 minutes

Servings: 4

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Ingredients for 4 servings.

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil, if needed
  • Lettuce, as desired
  • Tomato slices, as desired
  • Cheese and condiments, as desired

Seasonings and toppings can be adjusted to taste.

Equipment

Gather your tools before you begin.

  • Mixing bowl
  • Measuring spoons
  • Measuring scale
  • Spatula
  • Skillet or grill
  • Cutting board
  • Knife
  • Meat thermometer
  • Plate for serving

Kitchen Tip

Keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat ingredients and wash your hands and tools after handling raw meat.

Directions

Follow these steps to prepare your hamburgers.

  1. Prepare your ingredients. Wash your produce, slice your toppings, and set out your buns and condiments.
  2. Season the beef. Place the ground beef in a mixing bowl. Add the salt and black pepper, then mix gently until the seasonings are distributed.
  3. Shape the patties. Divide the beef into four equal portions and gently shape each portion into a patty.
  4. Heat your cooking surface. Warm a skillet or grill over medium-high heat. Add a little cooking oil if needed.
  5. Cook the patties. Cook the patties, turning as needed. Use a food thermometer to confirm the center reaches 160°F (71°C) for ground beef.
  6. Prepare the buns. If desired, lightly toast the hamburger buns on the skillet or grill.
  7. Assemble your hamburgers. Place each cooked patty on a bun and add lettuce, tomato, cheese, and your favorite condiments.
  8. Serve and enjoy. Serve the hamburgers while warm and refrigerate leftovers promptly.
